Custom work

Request a commission.

Do you have a vision for a hand-painted pair of shoes or a hat? We also make canvases and wood works on a commission basis, and we take mural inquiries. We look forward to hearing from you.

What to include

Send a note with what you have in mind, reference images if you have them, a rough size, a deadline if there is one, and a budget range if you can share it. Custom shoes and hats typically take about two weeks from payment. Large commissions take about a month. Original paintings, custom, engraved, and commissioned pieces are final sale.

Email theorganicmechanicshop@gmail.com or use the form on the contact page.
