A pillow in the shape of your pet.

Have your pet — or anyone you love — made into a custom-shaped pillow. Soft, zipper-free, and printed from your photograph. Available in three sizes. This product ships within the United States only.

How to order

  1. Choose a size and start checkout on the product you want, or email first if you have questions.
  2. Email a high-resolution photo to theorganicmechanicshop@gmail.com with the subject line Custom shaped pillow.
  3. William will follow up with any cropping notes before the pillow is printed.

Sizes: S 10″ × 10″, M 16″ × 16″, L 22″ × 22″. Thickness about 3–5″. Print on one side, white on the reverse. Spot wash with warm water.
