The mechanic

A studio practice in paint, resin, and fire.

The Organic Mechanic is the shop of artist William Bowers. The work moves between visionary acrylic paintings, pyrography and live-edge sculpture, and jewelry that holds real pressed flowers in resin — then out onto clothing you can actually wear.
